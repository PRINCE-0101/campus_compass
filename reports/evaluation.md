TF-IDF learns lexical associations, not full intent or campus knowledge. Shared terms such as registration, certificate and workshop blur categories. Low vocabulary coverage and a broad other class are further limitations. Inspect the examples above before proposing changes.

## Limits and next experiment
Scores are exploratory: the dataset is small, authored in one session and English-only. Related topics can occur across splits; exact normalized duplicates are blocked, but this is not a paraphrase-group or unseen-topic benchmark. Collect independently written student queries, group paraphrases before splitting, label ambiguous cases with a second annotator, and evaluate on a NEW untouched test set.

Confidence is the model’s maximum predict_proba score, not a calibrated correctness guarantee. For a rejected query it refers to the original top model class, not the fallback other label. Unknown vocabulary is rejected independently. A low-confidence rule does not reliably detect every unknown intent.

The selected threshold was tuned on validation macro-F1 (including other); there is no separately measured open-world OOD recall. Do not interpret this as production readiness.
